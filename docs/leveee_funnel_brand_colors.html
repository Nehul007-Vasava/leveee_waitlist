How it works

Three points in a disagreement.

No lectures, no scores, no taking sides. One quiet suggestion in the seconds that decide how the rest of the evening goes.

1
Before you send

A pause, not a filter

When a message you're writing sounds heated, Leveee offers a version that still says what you mean, in a way the other person can hear.

2
When it happens again

It remembers the context

If an argument follows a pattern you've been through before, Leveee points it out and reminds you what helped last time.

3
After it's over

One step towards repair

Instead of replaying it for hours, you get one concrete next step that's in your control, with no blame on either of you.

Privacy and safety

Built for your most private moments.

We're asking to be present when things are hard. You should know exactly what that means.

What Leveee is

  • Only on your side. It never accesses your partner's phone.
  • Neutral. It doesn't decide who's right.
  • Optional every time. You decide what gets sent.
  • Yours to delete, whenever you choose.

What Leveee isn't

  • Therapy, or a replacement for it.
  • A way to monitor another person.
  • Designed for relationships involving fear, control or violence.
If you ever feel unsafe with your partner, an app isn't the answer. Please contact a local helpline or someone you trust.

Questions

Before you join.

Will my partner know I'm using Leveee?

No. Leveee only works on your phone and your side of the conversation. Whether you tell them is entirely up to you.

What does Leveee see?

Leveee looks at the message you're writing so it can notice when things are getting heated. We'll publish exactly how data is processed, stored and deleted before the beta opens.

Is this a replacement for couples therapy?

No. Leveee helps in small everyday moments. For deeper difficulties, a qualified therapist is the right support, and the two can work well together.

Does joining the waitlist cost anything?

No. Joining is free and we won't ask for payment details. We'll share pricing well before anyone is asked to pay.

When will I get access?

We're inviting people in small groups so everyone in the beta gets real attention. You'll receive an email as soon as your place opens.
