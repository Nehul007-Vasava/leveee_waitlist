
# Leveee Waitlist Funnel: Refined Blueprint

The original draft has a solid structure. Its weakness is that it sells features ("interception," "pattern detection") before it makes the visitor feel understood. People with relationship pain don't sign up because of a feature. They sign up because a page describes their last fight better than they could. So the funnel below leads with emotion, then offers relief, then gives them one easy step.

## What changes, and why

1. **Lead with the moment, not the product.** "Catch the angry text" casts the visitor as the problem. It's better to describe the moment they already hate: thumb over send, heart pounding.
2. **Drop "in 14 days."** A pre-launch product can't back up a timed promise. In a trust-sensitive category, one unprovable claim makes everything else on the page look suspect.
3. **Handle privacy early.** Most visitors will wonder: "An app that watches my texts? Does my partner know?" If the page doesn't answer this, many will quietly leave.
4. **Ask the emotional question first in the modal.** Tapping "which one hurts most" is an easier first commitment than typing a name, and it gives you segmentation data.
5. **Rethink the viral loop.** People rarely share "I fight with my partner" with three friends. Invite-your-partner and gift framing fit this category better.
6. **Add a safety line.** A de-escalation tool must be clear that it isn't meant for abusive relationships. This is the right thing to do, and it also signals maturity to serious users.

## 1. Landing page, section by section

**Badge:** `Leveee • Private Beta, limited spots`

**Headline options to A/B test:**

- A: *"The fight isn't the problem. The 10 seconds before it is."*
- B: *"You've had this argument before. Leveee remembers, so you can stop having it."*
- C: *"For the moment your thumb is over 'send' and your heart is pounding."*

**Sub-headline:**

> Leveee is a private relationship companion. It notices when a conversation is starting to go sideways, and it gives you one small move before the damage is done.

**Primary CTA:** `Save my spot` (warmer than "Request VIP Waitlist Spot")
**Micro-copy under the button:** *Private. Your partner doesn't need to be involved. Takes 20 seconds.*

---

**Section 2: "Does this sound like you?"** (a mirror section)
Four short scenes written in the reader's inner voice. This is where visitors decide "this is for me."

- *It's 1am and you're still replaying what they said, and what you should've said.*
- *Same fight, different month. You can almost predict the next line.*
- *You said the one thing you promised yourself you wouldn't.*
- *By the time you're both calm, you're apologizing for* how *you said it, not* what *you meant.*

Close with: **"You're not bad at relationships. You're just alone in the moment that matters most."**

---

**Section 3: How Leveee helps** (three moments, not three features)

| Moment                              | What happens                                                                                                      | How it feels                                          |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Before you send**           | Leveee notices a heated draft and offers a pause, plus a rewrite that still says what you mean.                   | "I didn't have to swallow it. I just said it better." |
| **When it's happened before** | It recognizes the pattern ("This looks like the March conversation about plans") and shows what helped last time. | "Finally, something remembers the whole story."       |
| **After it's over**           | It suggests one repair move that's in your control, without blaming either of you.                                | "I knew what to do next instead of spiraling."        |

---

**Section 4: What Leveee is, and isn't** (trust block)

- ✅ Works only on *your* side. It never reads your partner's device.
- ✅ Doesn't take sides or score who's right.
- ✅ [State your real data policy here: on-device processing, no selling data, deletion on request. Only claim what's true.]
- ❌ Not therapy, and not a replacement for it.
- ❌ Not for relationships involving fear, control or violence. *If that's your situation, please reach out to a local helpline.* (Link a resource relevant to your launch markets.)

---

**Section 5: Founder note** (3–4 sentences, with a photo)
A short personal story: why you built Leveee, and the moment you wished you'd had a pause button. On a waitlist page, a real person builds more trust than any feature list.

**Final CTA:** repeat `Save my spot` with the privacy micro-copy.

## 2. Opt-in modal: emotion first, details second

**Step 1 (one tap, no typing):**

> **"What hurts most right now?"**
>
> - 🔥 I say things in the heat of the moment I regret
> - 🔁 We keep having the same fight
> - 🌀 I overthink and replay fights for hours afterward
> - 🧊 We go quiet and distant instead of talking
> - ✍️ Something else (short text field)

**Step 2:**

> **"Got it. Where should we send your invite?"**
> First name · Email · `Save my spot →`

**Optional Step 3 (skippable, adds qualification):**

- How long have you been together? (<1 yr / 1–3 / 3–7 / 7+)
- Would your partner be open to using something like this? (Yes / Maybe / No / Haven't asked)

The partner-openness answer is valuable product data. It tells you whether Leveee should stay solo-first or become two-sided.

## 3. Thank-you page, personalized by their Step 1 answer

**Open with validation, before any video:**

> *"You picked 'We keep having the same fight.' You're in good company. It's the most common answer we get. And the fact that you can see the loop means you're already halfway out of it."*

Write one short validation line for each of the five answers. This is the moment when visitors feel most understood.

**Then, in this order:**

1. **2–3 minute prototype walkthrough.** Show one real scenario that matches their pain (a heated draft being paused, or a pattern being surfaced). A raw, honest demo beats a polished one here.
2. **Jump the queue.** Offer three options, ordered from easiest to highest intent:
   - **Map your loop (2 min):** 5 more questions about their last argument (trigger, time of day, who withdraws, what usually ends it). This becomes your richest research data, and you can promise them a personal "Loop Snapshot" at launch.
   - **Talk to the founder (15 min):** *"Tell me about your last fight. I'll tell you what we're building. First 50 calls get Alpha access."* Embed Calendly.
   - **Invite someone:** frame it as *"Invite your partner"* or *"Gift a spot to a friend who needs this,"* not "share with 3 friends."

## 4. Nurture emails after sign-up (5 emails over 14 days)

| Day | Subject idea                             | Purpose                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0   | "Your spot is saved (and a question)"    | Welcome, restate privacy, ask them to reply with one word describing their last fight. Replies also help deliverability. |
| 2   | "Why the same fight keeps coming back"   | Value email on argument loops, segmented by their Step 1 answer.                                                         |
| 5   | "The 10-second rule"                     | One technique they can use today, before the product exists. This builds trust.                                          |
| 9   | "What I learned from 50 couples' fights" | Founder insights from research calls, which also proves momentum.                                                        |
| 14  | "Alpha is opening. You're in batch X"    | Scarcity based on real queue position, plus the founder-call CTA again.                                                  |

## 5. What to measure

- **Visitor → Step 1 tap:** tests whether the hero and mirror section resonate.
- **Step 1 → email submit:** tests friction and privacy trust.
- **Thank-you page actions:** loop-map completions and call bookings show true high intent.
- **Pain distribution:** which of the five answers dominates should shape your MVP's first feature.
- **Partner-openness split:** informs the solo vs. two-sided product decision.
